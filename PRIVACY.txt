PRIVACY POLICY
Updated September 29, 2026

IN SHORT
- Live Kins ("the app") sends nothing to its maker, ferisooo (mez.ink/ferisooo).
- No accounts, no tracking, no ads.
- Your data stays on your computer. Only what the story needs goes to Kindroid and DeepSeek, using your own keys.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR COMPUTER
- Your keys (.env)
- Names and profile IDs (config.json)
- Your settings, places, and story (state.json)
- A log of what the app did (simulation.log)
- The app's browser, with your Kindroid login (kindroid-browser)
- Pictures you added (backgrounds)
Don't share these. Settings > App > Wipe everything deletes them.

WHAT GETS SENT, AND WHERE
- Kindroid: the Narrator's messages, your kin's setting, profile switches, and reading your chats.
- DeepSeek: what it needs to write the story (names, places, settings, memories, your kin's recent lines), and your Help questions.
- kindroid.ai: your login, when you use Find, the side character, or the Extras you turn on (journal entries, favorites, reactions, selfies, background).
- First start only: downloads Python, the app's parts, and its browser.
- mez.ink: only if you click the link.
Nothing else is sent anywhere.

OTHER COMPANIES
Kindroid, DeepSeek, and the download sites have their own privacy policies. The maker doesn't control them and isn't responsible for them.

YOUR CHOICES
- Delete everything: Settings > App > Wipe everything, or delete the app's folder.
- Data kept by Kindroid or DeepSeek: ask them.

AGE
Only for people old enough to use Kindroid and DeepSeek.

NO LIABILITY
As far as the law allows, the maker is not responsible for anything that happens to your data or privacy while you use this app, including what other services do with it.

CHANGES
This policy may change. The newest version comes with the app.
